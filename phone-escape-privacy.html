Privacy Policy

Phone Escape

This policy is written to match what the app actually does. For Phone Escape that makes it unusually short: the game has no networking code in it, so there is nothing it could send anywhere even if it wanted to.

In short

  • No accounts, no login, no name, email or phone number
  • No adverts, no analytics, no crash reporting, no trackers
  • No in-app purchases
  • The app requests no Android permissions at all
  • Your progress is saved on your phone and never leaves it

1. Information we collect

None. We do not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information, device identifiers, advertising IDs, location, contacts, photographs or usage data.

Phone Escape contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, no crash reporting and no social login. Nothing in the app communicates with any server operated by us or by anyone else.

2. What is stored on your device

Your progress is saved locally using Android’s standard app storage. It never leaves the device and is not visible to us. It consists of:

  • Which cases you have completed, and your best time for each
  • Your daily streak — a count, and the day of your last completed Daily Mystery
  • Your case file — lines of evidence you have uncovered, which later cases refer to
  • Your sound preference

None of it identifies you.

3. Permissions the app requests

None. Phone Escape declares no Android permissions — not internet, storage, camera, microphone, location or contacts. You can confirm this on the app’s Play Store listing under App permissions, which will read “No permissions required”.

The only outbound action in the whole game is the Privacy Policy button in Settings, which hands this address to your own browser. The browser does the fetching; the game never touches the network itself.

4. Deleting your data

Open Settings from the top right of the main screen and choose Delete saved data. That erases every completion, best time, your streak and your case file from the device immediately. Uninstalling the app removes everything as well.

5. Children

Because the game collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from users of any age, as we do not collect personal information at all.

6. Your rights

Rights under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the CCPA and India’s DPDP Act — to access, correct, export or erase personal data — apply to data a company holds about you. We hold none. The only data that exists is on your own device, and erasing it is a button inside the game.

7. Changes to this policy

If a future version of Phone Escape ever collects anything — online leaderboards, for instance — this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described plainly here rather than buried.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can go to skyhopper089@gmail.com.
