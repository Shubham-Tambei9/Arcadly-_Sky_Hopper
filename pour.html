About the Game

Pour is a ball sort puzzle with one promise most of the genre cannot make: every level can be solved. Tap a tube to lift its top colour, tap another to pour. Colours never mix, and every tube has a limit — so the order you empty them in is the whole puzzle.

The difficulty curve is generated rather than hand-listed, so the levels never run out. Each board is checked by a solver before it ever reaches you, and the par shown on screen is the shortest solution that exists, not an estimate — so a three-star run genuinely means you found the best line.

Sound is synthesised on the device rather than streamed or bundled, so the game stays small and there is no delay between a tap and the sound it makes. No account, no sign-up, and your progress stays on your phone.

How it plays

Three rules. Endless depth.

01

Tap to lift

Tap a tube to pick up every ball of the same colour sitting on top of it.

02

Tap to pour

Pour onto a matching colour or into an empty tube. Colours never mix.

03

Sort them all

Every tube holds one colour, or nothing at all. Capacity is the constraint.

The curve

It never runs out.

Levels are not a hand-written list that stops at 500. Every knob is a function of the level number, so the difficulty keeps climbing for as long as you keep playing.
