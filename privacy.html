Sky Hopper

This Privacy Policy explains how Sky Hopper (we, our, or us) handles information when you use our Android game.

Sky Hopper uses third-party advertising services to display ads within the game. These advertising networks may automatically collect certain information from your device for advertising and analytics purposes.

Ads shown in the game may be personalized or non-personalized depending on the consent of the user, location, and device advertising settings.

2. GDPR & European users

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), we comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Users located in the EEA may be presented with a consent request before personalized advertisements are shown. You may choose whether to allow personalized or non-personalized ads, and withdraw consent at any time through your device settings.

Our app may use the advertising identifier of your device (such as the Google Advertising ID) to show relevant advertisements. You can reset or limit this through your device settings.

Sky Hopper is designed for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.
