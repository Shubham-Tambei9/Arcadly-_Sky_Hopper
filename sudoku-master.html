About the Game

Most sudoku apps decide difficulty by counting blank cells. It is the wrong measure — a board with fifty blanks can fall apart in two minutes while one with thirty fights back for an hour. Sudoku Master rates a puzzle by the solving techniques it actually requires, so an Expert board is genuinely expert.

Every puzzle is generated with exactly one solution and is solvable without ever guessing. If you are stuck, the answer is always somewhere on the board.

And when you cannot find it, the hints teach rather than tell. They come in three stages — where to look, why the logic works, then the answer — and the Sudoku Academy turns those same techniques into a proper course, from naked singles up to the patterns that crack the hardest grids.

The hints

Three taps. Three answers.

A hint that just fills in a cell teaches you nothing. Each stage gives away only as much as you ask for.

01

Locate

Points at the region worth looking at. Often that is all you needed.

02

Explain

Names the technique and walks the deduction, so you can finish the step yourself.

03

Reveal

Fills the cell in — the last resort, not the first.
