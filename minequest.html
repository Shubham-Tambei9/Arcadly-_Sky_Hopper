About the Game

Everyone who has played minesweeper knows the moment: the board is clean, the logic is exhausted, and two tiles are left with no way to tell which one kills you. MineQuest removes that moment. Boards are generated and then verified, so a chain of pure deduction always runs from your first tap to the last flag.

On top of that sits everything the original never had: a Daily Challenge that hands the same board to everyone, streaks to defend, achievements to unlock, an Endless mode for when one board is not enough, and global leaderboards to climb. Progress syncs to the cloud, so your streak survives a new phone.

Stuck anyway? The hint engine works out the deduction you missed rather than simply revealing a square — you finish the board having learned the pattern.

How it plays

Old rules. No coin flips.

01

Tap to reveal

Open a tile. Each number counts the mines touching it — that is your whole information budget.

02

Long-press to flag

Mark what you have proven is a mine. Your flags are the record of the reasoning.

03

Clear it cleanly

Every safe tile is reachable by deduction. If you are stuck a hint exists — a guess never has to.
